# Autonomous Revenue Ops

Interactive reviewer surface for a production-oriented revenue-operations workflow.

The Space demonstrates the separation between probabilistic qualification and deterministic business authorization. Reviewer inputs simulate AI qualification fields; the policy engine decides whether the lead may be auto-routed, requires human review, needs more research, enters nurture, or is blocked.
